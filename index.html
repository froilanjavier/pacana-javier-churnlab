Interpretation boundaries

Patterns can prioritize investigation of contracts, onboarding, billing, and service experience. They cannot establish that changing a contract or adding a service prevents churn.

No geographic map, calendar forecast, calibrated customer-risk score, or savings estimate is provided. Those require data and validation beyond the supplied snapshot.

The all-stayed reference exposes class imbalance. Accuracy alone does not show whether a model finds churners.
